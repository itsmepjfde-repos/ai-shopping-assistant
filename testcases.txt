Shopping Assistant Edge-Case Test Queries
=========================================

Before testing, set ECOMM_MOCK_DATA=edge in ecomm-agents/.env and restart
the API server.

1. Query: Find a black leather jacket under $100.
   Tests: Ambiguous black/leather matches and budget boundary prices:
   $99.99, $100.00, and $100.01. Check which products are supported by the
   catalogue descriptions and whether the over-budget product is excluded.

2. Query: Find a black leather belt under $50.
   Tests: Whether a product priced exactly at the budget ($50.00) qualifies
   while the similar $50.01 product does not.

3. Query: Find a wallet under $40, and tell me which options are in stock.
   Tests: Similar wallet matches, different availability states, and positive
   versus low review ratings.

4. Query: Find a brown leather jacket; compare the availability and reviews.
   Tests: An out-of-stock match, negative review sentiment, and similar jacket
   descriptions.

5. Query: Find a purple silk hat under $100.
   Tests: No-match behavior. The edge catalogue has no hats or products
   described as purple or silk, so the assistant should explain that it could
   not find a supported match.

Note: Catalogue search currently returns products matching any query term,
not necessarily every criterion. Inspect the Streamlit sidebar's LLM Debug Log
to compare catalogue results and tool observations with the final response.
